CAPACITY CONNECT

An integrated Digital Capacity Building and Learning Management Portal established by the Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) and the India Meteorological Department (IMD) to foster specialized meteorological, oceanographic, and climate competencies across India.

Key MoES Domains

  • Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP)
  • Doppler Weather Radar (DWR)
  • Satellite & INSAT-3DR Products
  • Tropical Cyclone Early Warning
  • Agrometeorology & GKMS
  • Seismology & Tsunami Warning

Capacity Building Links

  • Competency Mapping Engine
  • Public Certificate Validator
  • Trainer & Trainee Registration
  • Secure Officer Login
